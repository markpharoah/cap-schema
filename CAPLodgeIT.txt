## Doctrine ratified this session

1. **Duplicate-TFN survivor rule.** LodgeIT does not enforce TFN uniqueness;
   CAP's alternate key does. Pre-scan groups duplicates; survivor keeps the
   TFN (Active beats Former, tie by first-in-file); non-survivors load
   WITHOUT TFN — identity and clientcode intact so relationship edges never
   lose an end. Belt-and-braces try/catch retries without TFN on 0x80060892.
   Findings table at exit = LodgeIT clean-up list. Three duplicate pairs
   found and suppressed.
2. **Directed edges, mirror-free storage.** The 15-option vocabulary is
   one-directional (…of). LodgeIT's bare-label mirrors ("Director" vs
   "Director Of") are import-time translations only: bare rows load
   ends-swapped; symmetric types (Spouse, Associated With) normalise to
   unordered pair; Parent Of canonicalises to Child of ends-swapped; dedupe
   on (from,to,type) after normalisation. One fact, one row; the mirror
   belongs to the VIEW layer (each end's screen renders the same row from
   its own perspective — record once with dates, display mirrored).
   LodgeIT's own drift proved the point: Spouse 69 (odd) = at least one
   marriage recorded one side only; Director 44/42 = 8 single-sided.

   scaffolding. The practice's own trust loads as a NORMAL entity,

   structurally special enters the spine.
